About Our School

Tongani Secondary School is located in Pangani District, Tanga Region. The school offers Ordinary Level education and advance level and focuses on academic excellence, discipline, leadership, sports, environmental conservation, and community service. Our dedicated teachers and supportive administration ensure that students receive quality education and opportunities to develop their full potential.
